## 4. Interpretation boundary
The sources establish company-reported results, shipments, plans and industry statistics. They do **not** independently prove:
- future stock returns
- future valuation expansion
- customer diversification beyond disclosed information
- future HBM market share
- future pricing or margin sustainability
- that current CAPEX will avoid future oversupply

Those statements must remain JoyLab interpretation or scenario, not FACT.
