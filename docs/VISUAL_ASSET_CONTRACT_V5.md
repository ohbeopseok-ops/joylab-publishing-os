# JoyLab Visual Asset Contract V5

Status: Active candidate  
Version: 5.0  
Extends: Visual Asset Contract V4  
Scope: Website homepage, About, Research Archive, Books, Article visual assets, YouTube / Naver / social derivatives.

## 1. Purpose

V4 guarantees that article Hero, Supporting, and OG assets are technically valid and production-safe.

V5 adds the missing brand layer:

**One visual system across website, research, books, video thumbnails, blog covers, and print derivatives.**

V5 does not weaken any V4 image-quality rule.

## 2. Brand visual language

### Primary palette

- Deep Navy: `#041A3E`
- Electric Blue: `#2563FF`
- Cyan: `#00D1FF`
- Ivory: `#F8FAFC`
- Slate: `#94A3B8`
- Dark: `#090F16`

Accent colors may be used for semantic emphasis, but must not replace Deep Navy / Electric Blue / Cyan as the master identity.

### Typography

- Korean: Pretendard
- English / numbers: Inter
- Long-form Books may retain a serif editorial face when the book experience requires it.

### Signature language

Primary public message remains:

**분석에서 실행까지**

Extended positioning remains:

**생각을 분석하고, 분석을 실행으로.**

Visual micro-language:

**SIGNAL / EVIDENCE / DECISION**

Pillars:

**INVESTING × AI × LEADERSHIP**

Research method remains:

**Fact → Interpretation → Scenario → Action**

The micro-language supplements the Brand Identity Contract; it does not replace its canonical wording.

## 3. Surface rules

### Homepage

- Research Terminal × AI Operating System direction.
- Hero must use Deep Navy as dominant field.
- Electric Blue / Cyan are signal accents, not decorative gradients everywhere.
- Hero must remain inside the Homepage Vertical Budget.
- Editorial Top 3 remains readable in the first desktop viewport.
- Mobile 390px must remain overflow-free.

### About

- Light editorial canvas.
- Research-system structure must remain primary.
- Fact / Interpretation / Scenario / Action remains explicit.
- Grid/data cues may be used subtly; the page must not become a second dark landing page.

### Research Archive

- Dark research-terminal introduction is permitted.
- Archive cards remain light and readable.
- Research hierarchy and search/navigation usability take priority over decoration.

### Books

- Books may retain warm editorial / literary color and serif typography.
- Shared JoyLab identity should appear through navigation, signal accents, labels, and CTAs.
- Do not force all book covers or reading experiences into the dark research-terminal treatment.

### Article assets

All V4 Hero / Supporting / OG technical requirements remain mandatory.

## 4. Cross-channel derivative rules

### YouTube

- 16:9 master.
- One dominant subject.
- Title must remain legible at mobile thumbnail size.
- JoyLab mark and Electric Blue/Cyan signal accents should be consistent.
- Avoid generic neon-AI visual overload.

### Naver Blog / social

- Recompose for platform ratio; do not merely crop the YouTube master.
- 1:1 and 4:5 derivatives may change type scale and subject crop.
- Preserve the same title hierarchy and brand signal device.

### Print

- Convert RGB palette to printer-approved CMYK values.
- Digital glow effects must not be treated as equivalent in CMYK.
- Use printer proof for critical brand-blue reproduction.

## 5. Signature device

Approved recurring devices:

1. Deep Navy field
2. Electric Blue / Cyan signal line
3. Data grid or coordinate structure
4. Research / market / system imagery
5. Small uppercase research labels
6. SIGNAL / EVIDENCE / DECISION micro-language

Do not require all six devices on every artifact. Use 2–4 depending on the surface.

## 6. Prohibited patterns

- Generic purple-only AI gradients as the main identity
- Excessive neon/glow
- Multiple unrelated accent colors on one surface
- Decorative charts with fabricated data presented as real
- Replacing official JoyLab wording with campaign copy
- Forcing Books into the same visual density as Research
- Shrinking one design mechanically into every social ratio

## 7. V5 executable gate

V5 must verify:

1. V4 article asset gate remains GREEN.
2. Homepage contains approved brand micro-language.
3. Homepage, About, Research Archive, and Books load the approved V1.1 brand surface layer where required.
4. V5 palette tokens are present in the brand styles.
5. Canonical JoyLab brand positioning is not replaced.
6. Mobile and responsive visual gates remain independent mandatory gates.

## 8. Change control

A change to master palette, canonical message, Pillars, visual micro-language, or cross-channel signature device requires:

- before/after visual evidence,
- explicit contract update,
- affected-surface QA,
- rollback path.

## 9. GOLD

V5 GOLD requires:

- V5 contract PASS,
- Build PASS,
- relevant visual/mobile gates PASS,
- production deployment PASS,
- production smoke on affected live URLs.

A source change or PR alone is not GOLD.
