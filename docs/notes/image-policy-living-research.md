# Living Research Image Policy

Every publishable JoyLab Living Research article should ship with at least:

1. Hero image that communicates the thesis at a glance.
2. One explanatory infographic for the causal/transmission framework.
3. One data chart when the article contains a material quantitative comparison.

Images should use JoyLab visual identity (Deep Navy #0B1F4D, Electric Blue #1677FF, White), include meaningful alt text, and be stored under `public/images/research/` with stable article-oriented filenames.

Editorial done criteria now include image presence before merge.
